# Test-fit stand-ins (print these until the real parts arrive)

Printed stand-ins for HandUMI's bought motion parts, to check fits and assemble the mechanism before the real
bearings and rods arrive. They slide with more friction than the real parts and wear in: use them for fit and
range-of-motion checks, not for data collection. Generated by `tools/test_fit/make_test_fit.py`.

Everything in one download: [`test_fit.zip`](test_fit.zip).

| File | Stands in for | Print | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `lm4uu_standin_od7p9.step`, `_od8p0`, `_od8p1` | LM4UU linear bearing (8 × 4 × 12 mm), 4 per device | upright, 100% infill, PETG or PLA | Print all three. Keep the one that presses into the finger link's seat like the real bearing. The 4.35 mm bore slides on a Ø4 rod. |
| `mr63zz_standin_od5p9`, `_od6p0`, `_od6p1` | MR63ZZ ball bearing (6 × 3 × 2.5 mm), 4 per device | flat, 100% infill | Rings for the linkage pivots, with a 3.2 mm bore for the M3 screw. Keep the one that fits the seats. |
| `rod_gauge_d4p1_l135.step` | Ø4 × 135 mm rod, 2 per device | lying on its flat | Ø4.1, sand to fit. A printed 4 mm rod bends: use it to check the holes and the length only. A 135 mm piece of **M4 threaded rod** (any hardware store) is the better stand-in. |
| `pin_gauges_d3p9_to_4p2.step` | none | upright, on its tag | Four 30 mm pins, Ø3.9 / 4.0 / 4.1 / 4.2. Shows how your printer sizes Ø4 holes and pins. |

Printers usually make holes a little small and pins a little large. Print the gauges first and pick sizes from them.
