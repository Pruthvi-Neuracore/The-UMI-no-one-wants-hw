# Scissor-UMI · 2f-85: assembly

IKEA-style, drawn from the CAD: new parts in blue, pulled out along the way they go in; parts already built in grey; screws and dowels at their real holes, with the hardware for each step (to scale) at the top. All pages as one PDF: [`assembly.pdf`](assembly.pdf).

Drawn for the right-hand build. The left-hand build is the same with `handle_a_lh` / `handle_b_lh`, `grip_tpu_*_lh` and `quest_arm_left` (palm bar and finger loop swap sides).

Tools: soldering iron (heat-set inserts), 2.5 / 2 / 1.5 mm hex keys, small Phillips, CA glue, threadlocker, a vice or arbor press for the dowels.
